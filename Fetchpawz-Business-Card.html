    Saving the PDF
    1. Use Chrome or Edge. Safari ignores the card's page size.
    2. Print, then set Destination to Save as PDF.
    3. Margins None, Scale 100. The page size comes from the file: 3.75 × 2.25 in.
    4. You get two pages: the front, then the back.
    For the print shop
    • Size: 3.5 × 2 in trimmed. Each page is 3.75 × 2.25 in, with 0.125 in of bleed on every edge and no crop marks, which is what most online printers ask for.
    • Stock: 16 pt (350 gsm) or heavier. A coated matte or silk finish keeps Ball Orange bright; uncoated paper dulls it.
    • Corners: rounded, 1/8 in, if they offer it. The brand is soft, never sharp.
    • Colour: the PDF is RGB: Ball Orange #FF6A1F, Leash Navy #1C1F45, Biscuit #FFF2E3. Bright orange is the colour a press is most likely to dull, so ask for a printed proof before a big run.
    • Files: every font is embedded and the artwork is vector, so it stays sharp at any print resolution.
