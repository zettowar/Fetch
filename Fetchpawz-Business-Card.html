🐾 Fetchpawz business card — fill in & print

Every highlighted field is click-to-edit — click the name, title, email, or phone on the front and type over it.

  1. Edit the fields on the front card (the back needs no edits).
  2. Print with ⌘P → Save as PDF. Set margins to None, scale 100%, and enable Background graphics.
  3. Send the 2-page PDF to your printer: page 1 = front, page 2 = back.

The file is 3.75 × 2.25 in (3.5 × 2 in trim + ⅛ in bleed). The blue dashed line is the trim (cut) line, the green dashed line the safe zone — both are screen-only and won't print.
